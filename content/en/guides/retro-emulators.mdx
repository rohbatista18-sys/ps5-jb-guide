## What can you emulate?

With a jailbroken PS5 you can run emulators for retro consoles such as NES, SNES, Game Boy, Mega Drive/Genesis, PlayStation 1, N64, and more. Performance depends on the emulator and the system being emulated.

## How to set it up

<Step n={1} title="Get the emulator PKG">

Download the emulator from its official GitHub repository. It comes as a PKG file. Check the [verified repositories](/en/fw/7.00-13.60) list for reviewed emulators.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Install the emulator">

Install the PKG on the PS5. See [how to install PKG files](/en/guias/install-packages).

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Transfer your ROMs">

Copy your ROM files to the PS5 using [FTP](/en/guias/ftp-setup) or a USB drive. Place them in the folder the emulator expects (check the emulator's README).

</Step>

<Step n={4} title="Launch and play">

Open the emulator from the home screen, browse to your ROMs folder, and select a game. Most emulators support the DualSense controller natively.

</Step>
