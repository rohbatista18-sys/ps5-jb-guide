<Warn kind="terms" title="Your own games only">

</Warn>

## Why back up your games?

- Preserve your disc games as digital copies so you do not need the disc every time.
- Keep a copy in case a disc gets scratched or damaged.
- Store games on an external drive for faster access.

## How to back up a disc game

<Step n={1} title="Insert the disc">

Put the game disc in the PS5. Do not start the game.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Use a dumping tool">

With the jailbreak and HEN active, run a disc dumping homebrew app. It reads the disc and saves a copy to the console's internal storage or a USB drive.

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Transfer the backup">

Use [FTP](/en/guias/ftp-setup) or a USB drive to move the backup to your computer or external storage.

</Step>

<Step n={4} title="Install the backup">

The backup is a PKG file. Install it like any other PKG — see [how to install PKG files](/en/guias/install-packages).

</Step>

<Warn kind="ban" title="Do not share your backups">

Sharing your game backups with others is piracy. This guide exists to help you preserve your own library, not to distribute games.

</Warn>
