<Step n={1} title="Open Console Information" src="sony-version-en">

On the PS5 home screen, go to:

<MenuPath items={["Settings", "System", "System Software", "Console Information"]} />

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Write down the version" src="sony-version-en">

That screen shows the system software version, for example `9.60`. Type it in the box on this page and the guide opens the page for your version.

If "Up to date" appears under the version, your console already has the newest version Sony offers.

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Do not update while you are there">

Do not choose any update option on these screens. Read [the risks](/en/riscos) before you change anything.

</Step>
