## What it means

Y2JB has account requirements. Its README says it needs a fake-activated account, and that an account already activated on PSN cannot be used with it directly: you have to create and use a separate fake-activated account. Linking an account through PSN is not the same thing.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Read the account activation section of the Y2JB README" src="y2jb-readme">

The README describes creating a new offline account and fake-activating it. This site's [Y2JB guide](/en/guias/y2jb-autoloader) shows the same steps.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="ban" title="Leave your main account alone">

Do not assume that linking an account through PSN replaces the preparation Y2JB asks for. Do not change your main account unless you really need to.

</Warn>
