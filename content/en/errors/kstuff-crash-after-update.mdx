## What it means

Users report that on firmware 12.70, after updating to kstuff-lite 1.11, the exploit finishes but the console shuts down when the updated payload loads. This comes from a report on the kstuff-lite issue tracker. It is not confirmed for every console or firmware.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Compare your versions and logs with the report" src="kstuff-lite-issue-81">

The report is about firmware 12.70 with kstuff-lite 1.11. Write down the version of each part you are running and compare.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="As a check, go back to your last working combination">

As a check, try the last combination of versions that worked and is compatible with your firmware, changing only one part at a time.

</Step>
