## What it means

Sony says the USB storage device cannot be recognized.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Unplug the drive and plug it in again, all the way" src="sony-ce-109737-7" />

<Step n={2} title="Remove any USB hub and use another port" src="sony-ce-109737-7" />

<Step n={3} title="Try another USB drive" src="sony-ce-109737-7" />

<Step n={4} title="If it only happens in one app, reinstall that app" src="sony-ce-109737-7" />

## When to stop

<Warn kind="brick" title="Protect your files">

Never unplug a drive while the console is copying to it.
