## What it means

This is about the Lapse payload in Y2JB. The Y2JB README says Lapse only works up to firmware 10.01. On a higher firmware, the YouTube entry can still run, but the Lapse kernel payload does not complete.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Do not keep repeating Lapse on a higher firmware" src="y2jb-readme">

Trying again will not get past this limit.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Choose a chain that states support for your exact firmware">

Check its requirements too.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="terms" title="Lapse is not Relapse">

The documented limit for Lapse is 10.01. Do not confuse Lapse with Relapse, which is a different exploit.

</Warn>
