## What it means

ShadowMountPlus reads a game's details from `param.json` or `param.sfo` inside the `sce_sys` folder. When the log reports `missing/invalid param.json/param.sfo`, it cannot recognize the title.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Check that sce_sys is where it should be" src="shadowmountplus-readme">

For a game folder, the file must be at `sce_sys/param.json` or `sce_sys/param.sfo` inside that folder. For an image, `sce_sys` must be at the root of the image.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Look for an extra folder at the image root" src="shadowmountplus-readme">

An extra top-level folder inside the image can stop the title from being recognized.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="terms" title="Do not make up metadata">

Do not create made-up metadata files. Make the copy again from your own intact original.

</Warn>
