## What it means

The first time ShadowMountPlus scans your own game backups, it has to register every title. The README says this first scan can take longer if you have a large library, and that very large games need extra time to be checked.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Wait for the first scan to finish" src="shadowmountplus-readme" />

<Step n={2} title="Read the log before you restart the mounter" src="shadowmountplus-readme">

Debug logging is on by default (`debug=1`). Check `/data/shadowmount/debug.log` and the ShadowMountPlus notifications to see whether the scan is still moving before you restart anything.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="terms" title="Slow is not the same as frozen">

A slow first scan, on its own, does not prove that ShadowMountPlus froze. Look at the log before you decide.

</Warn>
