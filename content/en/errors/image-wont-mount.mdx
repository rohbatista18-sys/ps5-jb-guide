## What it means

ShadowMountPlus rejects your backup image, or the mount fails, sometimes with `broken pipe`. There is also a user report on the ShadowMountPlus issue tracker of exFAT images failing to mount with `broken pipe` on one build.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Check that the image is complete and the extension matches its format" src="shadowmountplus-readme">

`.ffpkg` is UFS, `.exfat` is exFAT and `.ffpfs` is PFS.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Compare your build with the report" src="shadowmountplus-issue-47">

The report describes exFAT images failing with `broken pipe` on a specific ShadowMountPlus build. Check which build you are running and compare.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="terms" title="Changing the extension does not convert the image">

Renaming only the extension does not turn the image into another format.

</Warn>
