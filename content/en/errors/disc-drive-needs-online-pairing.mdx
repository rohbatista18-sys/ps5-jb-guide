## What it means

Sony says the removable disc drive works only with the CFI-2000/7000 model group, and that an internet connection is required to pair the drive with the console when you set it up. Users report that, after a format or a reset, the console asks to pair the drive again, which they cannot do offline.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Check that it is the removable drive for the CFI-2000/7000 models" src="sony-disc-drive-en" />

<Step n={2} title="Check that the drive is installed as Sony shows" src="sony-disc-drive-en">

Sony's steps: turn the console off, unplug it and let it cool, then line up the drive's clips with the two marked holes and push until you hear a click. Put the cover and the stand feet back, reconnect the cables and turn the console on.

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Know that pairing needs the internet" src="sony-disc-drive-en">

Sony says an internet connection is required to pair the drive and the console at setup. Before you connect, make sure automatic updates are off, and stop if the console asks you to update.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="update" title="No offline way confirmed">

This guide found no confirmed offline way to pair this drive while keeping a low firmware. Connecting is your decision, and never accept a system update. If you get a disc error after a reinstall, also check the model, the game license and the disc itself: it does not automatically mean the laser is broken.

</Warn>
