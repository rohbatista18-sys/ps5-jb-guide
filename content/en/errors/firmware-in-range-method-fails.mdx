## What it means

A general range like 7.00 to 13.60 does not mean every version in it is supported the same way. Each implementation and build can have its own documented exceptions.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Check your exact firmware in the changelog" src="webkit-autoloader-changelog">

For example, the WebKit Autoloader v0.6.1 changelog (2026-10-08) notes that Relapse was still unsupported on 9.05 and 11.40 in that implementation, even though the general range is wider. Version 0.6.2 (2026-10-28) then added 11.40. Read the entry for the version you actually use, because the list changes between versions.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="terms" title="One build is not the whole story">

Do not treat an exception in one build as a permanent fact about that firmware.

</Warn>
