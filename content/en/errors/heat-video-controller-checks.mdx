## What it means

Users report several problems that look like hardware faults on jailbroken consoles: very high temperatures in games, stretched textures or black squares, a controller that asks for an update while the console stays offline, or a console stuck in a store demo mode. Each one needs its own checks. This page is a diagnostic order, not a documented fix.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="If it runs hot, stop and compare without payloads">

As a check, stop the game, make sure the vents are clear and air can flow, then try the same game without extra payloads.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="If several games show image glitches, check the video setup">

As a check, try another HDMI cable, another port and another screen, and try an original game with no changes.

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="If the screen stays blank, use Change Video Output in Safe Mode" src="sony-safemode-en">

Sony says this Safe Mode option changes the resolution or HDCP mode, and can help with a blank screen.

</Step>

<Step n={4} title="Update the controller from a PC" src="sony-dualsense-pair-en">

Sony says you can use a PC to update the DualSense controller software with the PlayStation Accessories app.

</Step>

<Step n={5} title="If the console is stuck in demo mode, back up and ask a repair service">

This guide has no confirmed way out of a store demo mode. As a check, back up your data and ask a repair service.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="terms" title="Do not jump to conclusions">

Do not decide the GPU is broken from one report, and do not set a fan curve at random. This guide found no safe way out of demo mode that works for everyone.

</Warn>
