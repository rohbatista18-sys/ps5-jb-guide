## What it means

On 13.60, BD-JB5 only works after the `bdj_unpatch` step. The BD-JB5 README warns not to reinstall the firmware, because that wipes the patch and you lose BD-JB. So access you had prepared can be gone after a reinstall of the system software.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Check whether the patch was removed" src="bdjb5-readme">

The project warns that reinstalling the firmware wipes the patch. If BD-JB stopped working right after a reinstall, that is the likely reason.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="If you have another compatible way in, plan the setup through it" src="bdjb5-readme">

The unpatch step needs a console that is already jailbroken, so it depends on having another entry point that works on your firmware.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="brick" title="Do not count on the patch surviving">

Do not assume the patch survives a firmware reinstall, or that you can apply it again without another way in. A reinstall can cost you BD-JB.

</Warn>
