## What it means

The etaHEN Toolbox has a Lite Mode option. Its description says it kills all etaHEN services and plugins, leaving a kstuff or HEN-only mode. The confirmation text adds that homebrew that needs etaHEN will also stop working. So it is expected that services or plugins stop after you switch it on.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Check what Lite Mode turns off" src="etahen-toolbox-options">

The option forcefully kills active services and plugins. Only the ELF loader stays running, so things can be turned back on.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Turn the features you need back on the way your build describes" src="etahen-toolbox-options">

Anything that depends on those services stays off until you re-enable it with your etaHEN build's own procedure.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="terms" title="Lite Mode does not survive rest mode">

The option text says the Lite menu will not survive rest mode. Do not count on it staying the same after the console goes into rest mode.

</Warn>
