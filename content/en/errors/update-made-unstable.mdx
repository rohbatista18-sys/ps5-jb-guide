## What it means

This is about autoloaders and payloads. Users report that a working setup got worse after changing versions: for example, freezes and shutdowns after moving to WebKit Autoloader 0.4.0, and shutdowns after a kstuff-lite update on firmware 12.70. These are reports for specific combinations, not confirmed for every console.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Write down which component you changed" />

<Step n={2} title="As a check, change one thing at a time">

Keep your settings and logs, so you can compare.

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Go back to the previous version only if it still supports your firmware" src="webkit-autoloader-feedback-040">

In the 0.4.0 discussion, several users say going back to 0.3.1 made things stable again.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="update" title="Do not downgrade the firmware">

Do not downgrade the console firmware to test this. A regression reported for one combination does not apply to every console.

</Warn>
