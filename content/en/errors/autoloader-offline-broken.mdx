## What it means

This is about the WebKit Autoloader. Users report that the saved cache can get damaged or lost, and the project has released fixes for cache problems. These are reports, not confirmed for every console or firmware.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Run the installer again to rebuild the cache" src="webkit-autoloader-releases">

Follow the steps for the release you use.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="As a check, see whether your chain needs a local network">

Some chains need a network connection even without internet. For example, the v0.5.1 release notes say Relapse needs an active Wi-Fi or Ethernet connection, even without internet access.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="terms" title="An offline page is not an offline chain">

A page saved for offline use and a whole chain that works with no network at all are different things. Check what your version supports.

</Warn>
