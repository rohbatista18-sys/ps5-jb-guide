## What it means

This is about HEN and other payloads. An exploit that works on your firmware does not mean every payload does: each ELF has its own firmware support. This needs checking for each payload and firmware, and it is not confirmed for every console.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Check each ELF's support in its release notes" src="etahen-releases" />

<Step n={2} title="Tell official releases apart from experimental ports" src="etahen-port-releases">

For example, a community port of etaHEN says on its releases page that it is unofficial and not an official etaHEN release, and some of its builds are marked experimental.

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="As a check, load only the components you need first">

Add optional services only after that works.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="terms" title="An unofficial port is not the official project">

Support announced by an unofficial port should not be credited to the official project.

</Warn>
