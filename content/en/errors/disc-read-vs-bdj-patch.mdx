## What it means

When a disc does not read on a jailbroken console, the cause can be the drive itself, a drive that is not paired, or the state of a BD-J patch. These are different problems. This page helps you tell them apart. It is a diagnostic order, not a documented fix.

## What to do

<Step n={1} title="Test a disc you know works">

As a check, put the disc in the right way up, and also try another type of disc.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="If your drive is removable, check that it is paired" src="sony-disc-drive-en">

Sony says the removable drive is for the CFI-2000/7000 models and must be paired online at setup. See [The disc drive needs to be paired online](/en/erros/disc-drive-needs-online-pairing).

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="For BD-J, check the patch state and restart between tests">

As a check, find out whether the BD-J port you use is patched or unpatched, and restart the console before each test. See [A BD-J disc shows a black screen or stopped working](/en/erros/bdj-check-patch-state).

</Step>

<Step n={4} title="If normal discs fail too, treat it as hardware">

As a check, if games or videos on disc also fail, contact PlayStation Support about the drive.

</Step>

## When to stop

<Warn kind="terms" title="Different discs, different paths">

A video DVD, a game disc and an exploit disc go through different paths in the console. No payload is a universal fix for the drive. If normal discs also fail, follow Sony's support for the hardware.

</Warn>
