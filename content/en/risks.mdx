Read this before you follow any tutorial. It is short on purpose.

<Warn kind="ban" title="Stay offline">

Do not sign in to PSN, and do not leave the console online, while it is jailbroken. Using PSN on a modified console can get the console or the account banned, and this guide cannot undo a ban.

</Warn>

<Warn kind="update" title="Do not update">

Every jailbreak works only on specific firmware versions. Updating can close it for your console, and you cannot count on going back.

- Turn off automatic downloads and installs of system software.
- Ignore every update prompt, including ones that show up when you insert a disc or open an app.
- Before you use any Safe Mode option, read what it does on Sony's support site.

</Warn>

<Warn kind="brick" title="Protect your data">

- Back up your saves before you start.
- Never unplug the console while it is writing data: updating, installing or copying.
- If something looks wrong, stop. Read the README and the open issues of the project you are using before you try again.

</Warn>

<Warn kind="terms" title="Warranty and terms">

Jailbreaking goes against Sony's terms of use and may void your warranty. You do it on your own console and at your own risk.

</Warn>

<Warn kind="scam" title="Scams">

- Nobody sells a jailbreak. A donation supports a developer; it never unlocks anything.
- Never run a program on your PC that promises to jailbreak your PS5.
- Only use the repositories listed on this guide, and only from their official GitHub page.

</Warn>

The exact Sony menu names and what each Safe Mode option erases are being checked against Sony's support pages. They will be added here with their sources.
